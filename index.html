Upload Tutorial

The Ganson has two categories, a gallery category and a "propaganda" category.


The gallery category is for officer(ganson) variants and other NAO (not a officer) memes related to the thy police, and posts must contain a NAO: or variant: tag. For example:

variant:officer propaganda:art nao:tommyparky

The propaganda(art) category accepts any otherwise unrelated but original content for propaganda/art themed posts, and they must be tagged media:battlefield, media:police, or media:military Or whatever your desired tag needs
